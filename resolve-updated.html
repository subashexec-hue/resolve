PROJECT 01 · WORKFLOW

AI Sales Opportunity Assistant

The Problem

Sales teams managing property enquiries handle repeated calls, WhatsApp conversations, meetings, site visits and follow-ups. Important context, next actions and follow-up dates can become scattered across manual notes and existing systems.

What We Built

A lightweight AI-assisted sales opportunity management prototype that converts salesperson interaction notes into structured opportunity intelligence and identifies what should happen next.

Workflow Architecture

SALES INTERACTION Customer interaction recorded (memo / notes)
↓
AI ANALYSIS
Requirement Sales stage Interaction summary Next action Follow-up date Waiting party Potential risk
↓
FOLLOW-UP ASSISTANT Contextual follow-up draft
↓
OPPORTUNITY DASHBOARD
Active · Upcoming · Overdue · Dormant

Validation

The prototype was tested using realistic real-estate sales interaction scenarios.

Important Note

This is a prototype demonstrating the workflow concept. It has not been validated using live company sales data and does not claim measurable revenue, conversion or productivity improvements.

PROJECT 02 · WORKFLOW

Hospital Compliance Intelligence

The Problem

Hospital planning requires architectural drawings to be checked against requirements spread across multiple healthcare planning, building, accessibility and safety sources.

What We Built

An AI-assisted first-pass screening and audit-support system designed to turn complex hospital drawings into structured compliance intelligence.

Workflow Architecture

HOSPITAL FLOOR PLAN PDF / drawing input
↓
DOCUMENT ANALYSIS Rendered page information · Embedded text · Visual layout information
↓
STRUCTURED RULEBOOK Healthcare · Building · Accessibility · Safety
↓
COMPLIANCE EVALUATION Rule matching · Conflict handling · Risk classification
↓
COMPLIANCE OUTPUT Findings · Risk indicators · References · Compliance ledger

Engineering Principle

“Confidence is not the goal. Traceability is.”

Validation

Tested against a real hospital drawing set from Suasth Hospital, Navi Mumbai. The first test surfaced weaknesses in classification, rule handling, compliance reporting and reconciliation transparency. Those findings were used to refine the system.

Important Limitation

This is an AI-assisted first-pass screening and audit-support system. It does not replace licensed engineering, architectural, regulatory or AHJ review.

PROJECT 03 · WORKFLOW

Healthcare Market Intelligence

The Problem

Healthcare business-development intelligence can require repeated manual research, analysis and report preparation. The challenge is turning scattered market activity into a structured and actionable intelligence workflow.

What We Built

An automated intelligence pipeline that finds, structures, stores and distributes healthcare market signals while connecting intelligence to an existing business-development workflow.

Workflow Architecture

01 / DISCOVER Healthcare market signals
↓
02 / ANALYSE AI-structured intelligence
↓
03 / REMEMBER Persistent intelligence record
↓
04 / ACT Email digest · ClickUp task

Core Idea

Information → Intelligence → Action

Validation

Benchmarked against real legacy reports previously produced manually by the analyst.

Important Note

This is an internal intelligence and decision-support workflow designed to augment human market analysis rather than replace human judgment.

CLIENT CASE STUDY

Internal Operations System

The Problem

Continuity Studios was managing task assignments, client updates, team communication and operational follow-ups through WhatsApp. Tasks could get lost in group chats, deadlines could slip and the founder had to manually chase the team to understand what was completed.

What We Built

A custom internal operations platform built around the agency's actual workflow. It includes: Separate admin and team-member portals, centralized task management, project and client tracking, in-app communication, automated email workflows, and role-based access.

Operational Workflow

TASK CREATED → ASSIGNED TO TEAM MEMBER → EMAIL NOTIFICATION → PROGRESS UPDATED → TASK COMPLETED → COMPLETION ALERT

Technical Stack

Base44 · Make.com · Webhooks · Automated Email

Outcome

A centralized professional workspace replaced fragmented operational communication through WhatsApp, giving the founder greater visibility into work and the team a structured place to manage tasks and communication.
